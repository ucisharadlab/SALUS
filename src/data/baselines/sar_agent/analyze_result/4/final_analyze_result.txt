Explanation: The question wants phone numbers of charter schools whose funding type is “Directly funded” and that opened after 2000-01-01. These attributes are all available in the schools table. The annotator’s query joins to frpm and filters there; since frpm has one record per school per academic year, the join multiplies rows, causing duplicates, and any directly-funded charter school lacking an frpm row is omitted. No DISTINCT or GROUP BY is used to remove the duplicates. Therefore the query is incorrect.
Revised:
SELECT DISTINCT `Phone`
FROM `schools`
WHERE `Charter` = 1
  AND `FundingType` = 'Directly funded'
  AND `OpenDate` > '2000-01-01';
