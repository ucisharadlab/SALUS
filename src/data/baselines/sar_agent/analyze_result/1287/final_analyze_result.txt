Explanation: The annotated query joins Patient with Laboratory and simply counts the joined rows that satisfy ALP < 300 and Admission = '-'. Because a patient can have multiple laboratory rows, the COUNT(T1.ID) counts one row per laboratory record, not one row per patient. The question asks for the number of patients, so duplicates must be eliminated (COUNT DISTINCT or GROUP BY). A comparison of the results shows 5,187 rows counted by the annotated query, but only 169 distinct patients meet the criteria. Other filter conditions (ALP threshold and outpatient status) are appropriate and the schema references are correct; only the handling of duplicates is wrong.
Revised:
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT T1.ID)
FROM Patient AS T1
JOIN Laboratory AS T2 ON T1.ID = T2.ID
WHERE T1.Admission = '-' AND T2.ALP < 300;
