Revised: If the goal is to list every card that has the ‘colorpie’ watermark along with its type, language and flavor text:
SELECT DISTINCT c.`type`,
       f.`language`,
       f.`flavorText`
FROM `cards` AS c
JOIN `foreign_data` AS f ON f.`uuid` = c.`uuid`
WHERE c.`watermark` = 'colorpie';
