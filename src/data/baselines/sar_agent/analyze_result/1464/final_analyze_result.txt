Correctness: No – the query forces every recipient to have matching rows in `attendance` (and therefore `event`). This requirement is not stated in the question and can incorrectly exclude students who did not attend any recorded event but still received funds. The extra joins are logically unnecessary.
Is_ambiguous: No – the question clearly asks for student full names and amounts for funds received on 2019-09-09.
Explanation: The needed data are in `income` (date_received, amount, link_to_member) and `member` (first_name, last_name, member_id). Inner-joining to `attendance` / `event` restricts results and may drop valid rows. `DISTINCT` is also unneeded once the superfluous joins are removed. Correct query:
SELECT m.first_name,
       m.last_name,
       i.amount
FROM income AS i
JOIN member AS m ON m.member_id = i.link_to_member
WHERE i.date_received = '2019-09-09';
Revised: SELECT m.first_name, m.last_name, i.amount FROM income AS i JOIN member AS m ON m.member_id = i.link_to_member WHERE i.date_received = '2019-09-09';
